LazyRelay vs Agorapulse

Agorapulse is a full social media management suite with a shared inbox, listening and reporting. Here's an honest look at where it and LazyRelay actually differ.

Where Agorapulse is genuinely ahead

Agorapulse does more than schedule. Its site describes a centralized social inbox, brand and competitor listening, reporting, a Social ROI feature, task assignment and shared content calendars. On its Advanced plan it adds multi-step approval workflows, moderation rules, and competitor benchmarking. It also supports X (Twitter), Reddit and Google Business, which LazyRelay doesn't (X is coming soon). If you need a serious inbox and reporting suite, Agorapulse is the better fit.

The gap that actually matters: "sent" isn't "published"

Agorapulse's pitch centers on engagement and reporting after you post. LazyRelay's is about the post itself: it separately re-checks the platform after every post to confirm it's genuinely live, instead of trusting the platform's first "accepted" response. That's Proof-of-Publish, included on every LazyRelay plan including Free. We didn't find an equivalent check on Agorapulse's pricing page or homepage.

The other real difference: per user versus flat

Agorapulse prices per user, so the bill grows with your team: the Standard plan is $79 per user per month on annual billing, with 10 social profiles. LazyRelay charges one flat price per tier, so Starter is $29.99/month for 20 connected accounts and 2 brands regardless of how many accounts you connect within the limit. LazyRelay also has a $0 Free plan, while Agorapulse offers a 30-day trial.

Pick Agorapulse if

  • You want a shared inbox, listening and reporting in the same tool as your scheduler.
  • You need multi-step approvals, moderation rules or competitor benchmarking.
  • You need X (Twitter), Reddit or Google Business Profile.

Pick LazyRelay if

  • You mainly schedule and publish, and $79 per user per month is more than you want to pay for that.
  • You want independent proof a post actually went live, not just "accepted."
  • You post to Telegram, Discord, Mastodon, Tumblr, WordPress, dev.to, Hashnode or Lemmy.
  • You want a free plan that doesn't expire, and MCP access for AI agents.

Try Proof-of-Publish for free. No card required on LazyRelay's free tier.

Frequently asked questions

Is LazyRelay cheaper than Agorapulse?

For scheduling, yes. Agorapulse's Standard plan is $79 per user per month on annual billing ($99 billed monthly) and includes 10 social profiles. LazyRelay's Starter plan is $29.99/month flat for 20 connected accounts and 2 brands. Agorapulse includes inbox, listening and reporting features that LazyRelay doesn't focus on, so it depends on what you need.

Does Agorapulse have a free plan?

We didn't find a free plan on agorapulse.com. It offers a 30-day free trial with no credit card required. LazyRelay has a $0 Free plan with 3 connected accounts and Proof-of-Publish included, no card required.

Does Agorapulse verify that a post actually went live, like LazyRelay's Proof-of-Publish?

Not that we found documented on agorapulse.com. LazyRelay's Proof-of-Publish independently re-checks each platform after publishing to confirm the post is genuinely live, on every plan including Free.
