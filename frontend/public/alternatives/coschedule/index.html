LazyRelay vs CoSchedule

CoSchedule is a marketing calendar first and a social scheduler second. Here is an honest look at where it and LazyRelay actually differ.

Where CoSchedule is genuinely ahead

CoSchedule keeps projects, campaigns, social posts and events on one shared calendar, so a whole marketing team can work from a single view. It also lets clients or an internal review team give final sign off before anything publishes. If a shared marketing calendar is what you are buying, that is CoSchedule's strength. LazyRelay is a scheduler and publisher, not a project calendar.

Pick CoSchedule if

  • You want social posts, campaigns and projects on one shared marketing calendar.
  • You need Google Business Profile posting, which LazyRelay does not offer today.
  • Your clients or internal reviewers sign off on every message before it goes out and you want that inside a marketing calendar.

Pick LazyRelay if

  • You want to post to Telegram, Discord or Tumblr, which are not on CoSchedule's list.
  • You want independent proof that a post actually went live, not only that it was accepted.
  • You post full length YouTube videos, not only Shorts.
  • You want AI agent access through MCP on every plan, including Free.

Frequently asked questions

Does CoSchedule post to Telegram, Discord or Tumblr?

Not on the list of networks in CoSchedule's help center, which names 11 networks. LazyRelay supports all three.

Does CoSchedule have a free plan?

Yes. Its pricing page shows a free plan with 1 profile and up to 15 scheduled posts at a time. LazyRelay's free tier has 3 accounts and no card is required.

Does CoSchedule check that a post is really live after publishing?

We did not find that on the pages we read. LazyRelay's Proof of Publish checks the platform again after every post, on every plan including Free.
