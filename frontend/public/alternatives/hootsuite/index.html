LazyRelay vs Hootsuite

Hootsuite is one of the biggest, longest-established names in social media management, built for larger teams. Here's an honest look at where it and LazyRelay actually differ.

Where Hootsuite is genuinely ahead

Hootsuite's "Wisdom" AI runs across every plan and advertises MCP connectors alongside in-app drafting and 90-day trend forecasting — a broader AI feature set than LazyRelay's, though we couldn't confirm from their pricing page which tier the MCP piece actually requires. Hootsuite also offers real team workflow tools (approvals, auto-routing messages, custom compliance) built for larger organizations, which is genuinely the market Hootsuite is built for and LazyRelay isn't trying to be.

The gap that actually matters: "sent" isn't "published"

Hootsuite's own marketing focuses on scheduling reliability and AI-assisted content, not on independently confirming a post is actually visible after it publishes. LazyRelay separately re-checks the platform after every post to confirm it's genuinely live — that's Proof-of-Publish, included on every LazyRelay plan including Free. We didn't find an equivalent check anywhere in Hootsuite's own documentation.

The other real difference: no free plan, and pricing per seat

Hootsuite charges per user, not per account — a 3-person team on Standard is $297/month before any account limits even come into play. LazyRelay is priced in credits, not per user: Growth is $29 a month for 1,000 credits. And Hootsuite has nothing between "pay $99/month" and "14-day trial". LazyRelay's Free plan has no time limit, and its 30 credits do not expire.

Pick Hootsuite if

  • You're a larger team that needs mature approval workflows, compliance tooling, and departmental organization out of the box.
  • Budget isn't the deciding factor and you want one of the most established names in the category.

Pick LazyRelay if

  • You want to try a real product for free, indefinitely, before paying anything.
  • You're a solo operator or small team — Hootsuite's per-seat pricing punishes exactly this use case.
  • You want independent proof a post actually went live, not just "accepted."
  • You post to Telegram, Discord, or Tumblr.

Try Proof-of-Publish for free. LazyRelay's free tier includes 30 credits once at registration.

Frequently asked questions

Is LazyRelay cheaper than Hootsuite?

Yes, substantially. Hootsuite's entry plan is $99/month per user for 10 social accounts, with no free plan, only a 14-day trial. LazyRelay's entry paid option is Pay-as-you-go at $15 one time for 300 credits, Growth is $29 a month, and its Free plan is $0 with 30 credits once at registration.

Does Hootsuite have a free plan?

No. Hootsuite removed its permanent free plan in March 2023 and now only offers a 14-day free trial, no credit card required. LazyRelay has a $0 Free plan with 30 credits once at registration, Proof-of-Publish and AI-agent/MCP access included.

Does Hootsuite verify that a post actually went live?

Not that we found documented on hootsuite.com. LazyRelay's Proof-of-Publish independently re-checks each platform after publishing to confirm the post is genuinely live, on every plan including Free.
