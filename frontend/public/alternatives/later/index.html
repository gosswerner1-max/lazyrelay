LazyRelay vs Later

Later started as an Instagram-first visual planner and has grown into a broader scheduler. Here's an honest look at where it and LazyRelay actually differ.

Where Later is genuinely ahead

Later's visual content calendar and Link in Bio tooling are more mature than LazyRelay's, and its AI content tools include future-trend forecasting on higher tiers. Later also supports Snapchat, which LazyRelay does not.

The gap that actually matters: "sent" isn't "published"

Later's own marketing focuses on visual planning and AI content tools, not on independently confirming a post is actually visible after it publishes. LazyRelay separately re-checks the platform after every post to confirm it's genuinely live — that's Proof-of-Publish, included on every LazyRelay plan including Free. We didn't find an equivalent check anywhere in Later's own documentation.

The other real difference: paying doesn't remove the posting cap

Later's entry paid plan still caps you at 30 posts per profile a month — a real limit that only lifts once you're paying $82.50/month for the Scale plan. LazyRelay's Starter plan at $29.99/month includes unlimited one-time scheduled posts from day one; the only volume limit that exists at all is on the free plan (10 posts per account, refillable).

Pick Later if

Frequently asked questions

Does Later have a free plan?

No. Later discontinued its free plan and now offers only a 14-day free trial. LazyRelay has a genuine $0-forever Free plan with Proof-of-Publish and AI-agent/MCP access included.

Does Later's cheapest plan have a posting limit?

Yes. Later's Starter plan caps you at 30 scheduled posts per profile per month, even though it's a paid plan. Unlimited posting requires the $82.50/month Scale plan. LazyRelay's paid plans include unlimited one-time scheduled posts from the entry Starter tier.

Does Later verify that a post actually went live, like LazyRelay's Proof-of-Publish?

Not that we found documented on later.com. LazyRelay's Proof-of-Publish independently re-checks each platform after publishing to confirm the post is genuinely live, on every plan including Free.
