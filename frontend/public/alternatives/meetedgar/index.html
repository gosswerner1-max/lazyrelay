LazyRelay vs MeetEdgar

MeetEdgar is built around a content library that it keeps recycling. Here is an honest look at where it and LazyRelay actually differ.

Where MeetEdgar is genuinely ahead

MeetEdgar keeps your content in an unlimited library and automatically re shares older posts when new content runs out. It also lets you add up to 20 team members on any plan at no extra cost. If you want evergreen content on autopilot, that library and recycling model is its strength, and LazyRelay does not work that way.

Pick MeetEdgar if

  • You want evergreen posts to repeat automatically from a library.
  • You need Google Business Profile posting, which LazyRelay does not offer today.
  • You want up to 20 team members included on any plan.

Pick LazyRelay if

  • You want to post to Telegram, Discord or Tumblr.
  • You want a free tier with no trial clock, and no card required.
  • You want approval workflows and client review links.
  • You want independent proof that a post actually went live.

Frequently asked questions

Does MeetEdgar have a free plan?

No. Its pricing page says it offers a 30 day free trial instead of a free plan. LazyRelay's free tier has no time limit.

What is MeetEdgar best known for?

An unlimited content library, and automatic re sharing of older posts when new content runs out, as its features page describes.

Does MeetEdgar check that a post is really live after publishing?

We did not find that on the pages we read. LazyRelay's Proof of Publish checks the platform again after every post, on every plan including Free.
