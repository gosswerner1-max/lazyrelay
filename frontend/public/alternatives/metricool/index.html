LazyRelay vs Metricool

Metricool pairs scheduling with real analytics and a genuine free plan. Here's an honest look at where it and LazyRelay actually differ.

Where Metricool is genuinely ahead

Metricool's free plan includes competitor tracking (5 profiles) and 30 days of analytics history — real, useful features LazyRelay's Free plan doesn't have. Metricool is also built analytics-first, with deeper reporting (PDF/PPT exports, Looker Studio connector) than LazyRelay currently offers.

The gap that actually matters: "sent" isn't "published"

Metricool is built around scheduling and analytics, not independently confirming a post is actually visible after it publishes. LazyRelay separately re-checks the platform after every post to confirm it's genuinely live — that's Proof-of-Publish, included on every LazyRelay plan including Free. We didn't find an equivalent check anywhere in Metricool's own documentation.

The other real difference: what the free plan actually lets you do

Metricool's free plan caps you at 20 posts a month total across every connected network, and cuts off LinkedIn and X entirely regardless of plan tier — you have to upgrade just to connect those platforms at all. LazyRelay's Free plan doesn't gate specific platforms behind a paywall (every plan, including Free, connects the same 12 platforms); the limit is 10 posts per connected account, which refill monthly, and one-time scheduling only (no recurring schedules on Free).

Pick Metricool if

  • Competitor tracking and deep analytics reporting matter more to you than scheduling volume.
  • You don't need LinkedIn or X, or you're fine paying extra for them.
  • You're running many brands and want per-feature add-ons rather than a single flat tier.

Pick LazyRelay if

  • You want every supported platform available on the free plan, not gated behind an upgrade.
  • You post to Telegram, Discord, or Tumblr.
  • You want independent proof a post actually went live, not just "accepted."
  • You'd rather have one flat price per tier than per-feature add-ons stacking up.

Try Proof-of-Publish for free. No card required on LazyRelay's free tier.

Frequently asked questions

Does Metricool's free plan include LinkedIn and X?

No. Metricool's free plan explicitly excludes LinkedIn and X (Twitter) entirely — both require a paid plan. LazyRelay's Free plan doesn't include LinkedIn or X either, but covers Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube, Mastodon, Bluesky, Telegram, Discord, and Tumblr on every plan including Free.

Does Metricool have AI-agent (MCP) access on its free plan?

Yes, and so does LazyRelay. Both companies include MCP access on their free plans.

Does Metricool verify that a post actually went live, like LazyRelay's Proof-of-Publish?

Not that we found documented on metricool.com. LazyRelay's Proof-of-Publish independently re-checks each platform after publishing to confirm the post is genuinely live.
