LazyRelay vs Planoly

Planoly is a visual social media planner with eight networks. Here is an honest look at where it and LazyRelay actually differ.

Where Planoly is genuinely ahead

Planoly splits publishing clearly: it posts automatically where a platform allows it and sends a reminder where the platform needs native posting. Its AI assistant connector also says it confirms before publishing or deleting anything. If you want that clear split between auto posting and reminders, that is its strength.

Frequently asked questions

Does Planoly post to X automatically?

No. Its help center says X is reminder only, so Planoly notifies you instead of posting. LazyRelay posts to X on paid plans, using your own X developer keys.

Does Planoly have MCP access for AI agents?

Yes, as an early alpha. Its AI page says the feature is not enabled for every account yet. LazyRelay includes 27 MCP tools on every plan, including Free.

Does Planoly have a free plan?

Its help center describes one: 1 workspace, 1 user and 10 posts per month per workspace, and says it is not in the plan picker. LazyRelay's free tier has 3 accounts and no card required.
