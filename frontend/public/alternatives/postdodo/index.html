LazyRelay vs PostDodo

Both tools verify that a scheduled post actually went live instead of stopping at "the platform accepted it." Here's an honest look at where they actually differ.

Where they're genuinely alike

PostDodo reads back the platform's own post link after publishing and saves it as a receipt. LazyRelay's Proof-of-Publish does the same core thing: a separate check after the post goes out, confirming it's actually visible rather than trusting the "accepted" response. If you're comparing these two specifically because of that promise, both of them keep it. This isn't a case where one tool made something up and the other is calling it out.

Pick PostDodo if

  • X (Twitter) is a core platform for you and you would rather not manage your own X developer keys and X's per-post charges.
  • You want the lowest entry price for a monthly plan and don't need a free tier.

Pick LazyRelay if

  • You want to try Proof-of-Publish for free before paying anything, with 30 credits to start.
  • You post to Telegram, Discord, or Tumblr and need those covered natively.
  • You manage more than one brand or client and want that built into the plan rather than a separate workspace.
  • You or your team want API or MCP access to automate scheduling.

Try Proof-of-Publish for free. LazyRelay's free tier includes 30 credits once at registration.

Frequently asked questions

Does PostDodo also verify that posts actually went live?

Yes. PostDodo reads back the platform's own post link after publishing and saves it as a receipt, which is the same basic idea as LazyRelay's Proof-of-Publish: confirming the post is genuinely live rather than trusting the platform's initial "accepted" response. This is a real strength PostDodo has, not something unique to LazyRelay.

Does LazyRelay support X (Twitter) like PostDodo does?

Yes, on any paid plan, but differently: you connect X with your own X developer keys and X bills your own developer account for each post, so X posting is not included in the plan price. PostDodo also supports X.

Is LazyRelay cheaper than PostDodo?

It depends on the plan. PostDodo's entry tier is $25/month versus LazyRelay's Growth at $29 a month, or Pay-as-you-go at $15 one time for 300 credits. But LazyRelay has a genuine free tier with 30 credits and Proof-of-Publish included, which PostDodo does not offer; PostDodo's trial requires a card upfront.
