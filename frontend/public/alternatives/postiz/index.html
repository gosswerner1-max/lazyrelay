LazyRelay vs Postiz

Postiz is an open-source scheduler with an AI copilot and a self-hostable option. Here's an honest look at where it and LazyRelay actually differ.

Where Postiz is genuinely ahead

Postiz is open-source and can be self-hosted for free if you have the technical capacity to run your own server — a real option LazyRelay doesn't offer. Its AI copilot also generates images and short videos directly (up to 60 AI videos/month on its top tier), which goes beyond LazyRelay's current AI features (captions and hashtag suggestions).

The gap that actually matters: "sent" isn't "published"

Postiz's own marketing focuses on AI content generation and channel volume, not on independently confirming a post is actually visible after it publishes. LazyRelay separately re-checks the platform after every post to confirm it's genuinely live — that's Proof-of-Publish, included on every LazyRelay plan including Free. We didn't find an equivalent check anywhere in Postiz's own documentation.

The other real difference: hosted free vs. host-it-yourself free

Postiz's "free" option means running your own server, which is a real cost in time and technical effort even if there's no subscription fee. LazyRelay's Free plan is hosted — sign up and it works, nothing to deploy or maintain.

Pick Postiz if

  • You have the technical capacity to self-host and want full control over your own instance.
  • Built-in AI image and video generation matter more to you than post verification.

Pick LazyRelay if

  • You want a genuinely hosted free plan — nothing to install or maintain.
  • You post to Telegram, Discord, Mastodon, Bluesky, or Tumblr.
  • You want independent proof a post actually went live, not just "accepted."

Try Proof-of-Publish for free. No card required on LazyRelay's free tier.

Frequently asked questions

Is Postiz free?

Only if you self-host it yourself, which requires technical setup and your own server. The hosted, cloud version has no free plan — it starts at $29/month with a 7-day trial. LazyRelay has a genuine hosted $0-forever Free plan, nothing to install or host yourself.

Is LazyRelay cheaper than Postiz?

It depends on account count. Postiz's Standard plan is $29/month for 5 channels, cheaper than LazyRelay's $29.99/month Starter at small scale. But LazyRelay's Starter covers 20 accounts for about the same price — Postiz needs its $49/month Pro plan to reach 30 channels.

Does Postiz verify that a post actually went live, like LazyRelay's Proof-of-Publish?

Not that we found documented on postiz.com. LazyRelay's Proof-of-Publish independently re-checks each platform after publishing to confirm the post is genuinely live, on every plan including Free.
