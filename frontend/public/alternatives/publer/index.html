LazyRelay vs Publer

Publer is a budget-friendly scheduler with a genuine free plan and low per-account paid pricing. Here's an honest look at where it and LazyRelay actually differ.

Where Publer is genuinely ahead

Publer's paid pricing is aggressively cheap — its Professional plan starts around $4/month and scales per account, with every 10th account free. For a solo operator on a tight budget who doesn't need X (Twitter) or a long post history, Publer's paid tiers can undercut LazyRelay significantly.

The gap that actually matters: "sent" isn't "published"

Publer's own marketing focuses on affordability and ease of use, not on independently confirming a post is actually visible after it publishes. LazyRelay separately re-checks the platform after every post to confirm it's genuinely live — that's Proof-of-Publish, included on every LazyRelay plan including Free. We didn't find an equivalent check anywhere in Publer's own documentation.

The other real difference: what "free" actually gives you

Publer's free plan looks similar to LazyRelay's on the surface — 3 accounts, 10 posts per account — but only keeps your published-post history for 24 hours, so there's no lasting record of what actually went out. LazyRelay's Free plan has no such time limit, and includes Proof-of-Publish verification and MCP/AI-agent access that Publer's free plan doesn't appear to offer at all.

Pick Publer if

  • Budget is the deciding factor and you're comfortable paying per account rather than a flat tier.
  • You need X (Twitter) and don't mind paying for it.

Pick LazyRelay if

  • You want your post history to actually stick around on the free plan, not disappear after 24 hours.
  • You post to Telegram, Discord, Mastodon, Bluesky, or Tumblr.
  • You want AI-agent/MCP access without needing to check whether it's even offered.
  • You want independent proof a post actually went live, not just "accepted."

Try Proof-of-Publish for free. No card required on LazyRelay's free tier.
