LazyRelay vs Publora

Publora positions itself as "the publishing API for the agent era," built API/MCP-first from the ground up. Here's an honest look at where it and LazyRelay actually differ.

Where Publora is genuinely ahead

Publora's per-account pricing is aggressive — graduated volume discounts bring the cost per account down as low as $0.99-$1.99/month at higher volumes, and API/MCP access is included at every tier, including Agency.

The gap that actually matters: "sent" isn't "published"

Publora's own positioning is entirely about being agent-native — API and MCP access on every tier, webhooks, an AI editor. What it doesn't advertise is independently confirming a post is actually visible after it publishes. LazyRelay separately re-checks the platform after every post to confirm it's genuinely live — that's Proof-of-Publish, included on every LazyRelay plan including Free. For a product built specifically for AI agents that publish without a human watching, that's arguably the more important gap to close, not less.

The other real difference: per-account math

Publora's graduated per-account pricing genuinely wins at small scale and stays competitive even at moderate volume — its own numbers claim accounts 21+ drop to $0.99/month each. LazyRelay's flat pricing doesn't scale down the same way, but it also doesn't require doing that math: $29.99 / mo (plus applicable tax) covers up to 20 accounts outright, no per-account calculation needed.

Pick Publora if

  • X (Twitter) is a core platform for you and you would rather not manage your own X developer keys and X's per-post charges.
  • You want per-account pricing that keeps dropping as you scale, and don't mind doing the math.

Pick LazyRelay if

  • You want independent proof a post actually went live, not just "accepted" — especially if an AI agent is the one posting unsupervised.
  • You post to Telegram, Discord, Mastodon, Bluesky, or Tumblr.
  • You'd rather have one flat price than calculate a per-account bill.

Try Proof-of-Publish for free. No card required on LazyRelay's free tier.

Frequently asked questions

LazyRelay's flat $29.99 / mo (plus applicable tax) Starter plan for 20 accounts becomes the better deal once you're running more than a handful of accounts.

Does Publora verify that a post actually went live, like LazyRelay's Proof-of-Publish?

Not that we found documented on publora.com. LazyRelay's Proof-of-Publish independently re-checks each platform after publishing to confirm the post is genuinely live.
