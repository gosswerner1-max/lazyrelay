LazyRelay vs Sendible

Sendible is aimed at agencies, with unlimited users and client workspaces. Here is an honest look at where it and LazyRelay actually differ.

Where Sendible is genuinely ahead

Sendible includes unlimited users on every plan, with no per seat fees, and can send one post to up to 50 profiles at once. It also supports separate workspaces for client brands. If you run an agency with a large team, that user model is its strength. LazyRelay includes multi brand workspaces too, with a cap per plan.

Pick Sendible if

  • You run an agency and want unlimited users on every plan.
  • You want to send one post to many profiles at once, up to 50 in one go.
  • You need Google Business Profile posting, which LazyRelay does not offer today.

Pick LazyRelay if

  • You want to post to Telegram, Discord or Tumblr.
  • You want a free tier with no trial clock, and no card required.
  • You want independent proof that a post actually went live.
  • You want AI agent access through MCP on every plan.

Try Proof of Publish for free. No card required on LazyRelay's free tier.

Frequently asked questions

Does Sendible have a free plan?

Its pricing page shows a 14 day free trial with no card required, and no permanent free plan. LazyRelay's free tier has no time limit.

How many users does Sendible include?

Its scheduler page says unlimited users on every plan, with no per seat fees. LazyRelay's team seats depend on your plan.

Does Sendible check that a post is really live after publishing?

We did not find that on its pricing or features pages. LazyRelay's Proof of Publish checks the platform again after every post, on every plan including Free.
