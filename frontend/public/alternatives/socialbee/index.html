LazyRelay vs SocialBee

SocialBee leans on content categories and recycling for solo operators and small teams. Here's an honest look at where it and LazyRelay actually differ.

Where SocialBee is genuinely ahead

SocialBee's content categories and content recycling (posting evergreen content on a repeating rotation) are more developed than anything LazyRelay currently offers, and its AI content generation is unlimited on every paid tier. For a solo operator running only 5 profiles, SocialBee's Bootstrap plan is also cheaper than LazyRelay's Starter.

The gap that actually matters: "sent" isn't "published"

SocialBee's own marketing focuses on content organization and recycling, not on independently confirming a post is actually visible after it publishes. LazyRelay separately re-checks the platform after every post to confirm it's genuinely live — that's Proof-of-Publish, included on every LazyRelay plan including Free. We didn't find an equivalent check anywhere in SocialBee's own documentation.

The other real difference: what happens as you add accounts

SocialBee's per-profile pricing is genuinely cheaper than LazyRelay's at very small scale (5 profiles). But it climbs through three tiers to get to 25 profiles at $82.50/month, while LazyRelay's $29.99/month Starter plan already covers 20 accounts flat. Where the crossover happens depends on exactly how many accounts you run — worth checking both pricing pages against your real numbers.

Pick SocialBee if

  • You're a solo operator running a handful of profiles and want the lowest entry price.
  • Content recycling and category-based organization are core to how you plan content.

Pick LazyRelay if

  • You want to try a real product for free, indefinitely, before paying anything.
  • You're running closer to 20 accounts, where flat pricing starts winning.
  • You post to Telegram, Discord, Mastodon, Bluesky, or Tumblr.
  • You want independent proof a post actually went live, not just "accepted."

Try Proof-of-Publish for free. No card required on LazyRelay's free tier.

Frequently asked questions

Does SocialBee have a free plan?

No. SocialBee offers a 14-day free trial with full Pro-plan access and no credit card required, but there is no permanent free plan. LazyRelay has a genuine $0-forever Free plan.

Is LazyRelay cheaper than SocialBee?

It depends on account count. SocialBee's Bootstrap plan is about $24.20/month billed annually for 5 profiles, cheaper than LazyRelay's $29.99/month Starter for smaller setups. LazyRelay's flat pricing tends to win once you're running closer to 20 accounts, which SocialBee's tiers don't reach until its $82.50/month Pro plan (25 profiles).

Does SocialBee verify that a post actually went live, like LazyRelay's Proof-of-Publish?

Not that we found documented on socialbee.com. LazyRelay's Proof-of-Publish independently re-checks each platform after publishing to confirm the post is genuinely live, on every plan including Free.
