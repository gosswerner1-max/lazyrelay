LazyRelay vs SocialPilot

SocialPilot is built for agencies and teams, with bulk scheduling and client approvals. Here is an honest look at where it and LazyRelay actually differ.

Where SocialPilot is genuinely ahead

SocialPilot can schedule up to 500 posts at once from a CSV file, and its higher plans add client approval workflows for agencies. It also runs a hosted MCP server that connects Claude and ChatGPT to your account, included on every plan, so LazyRelay is not the only scheduler with AI agent access. If bulk CSV scheduling is central to your work, SocialPilot is the stronger fit.

Pick SocialPilot if

  • You schedule posts in bulk from CSV files, up to 500 at a time.
  • You need Google Business Profile posting, which LazyRelay does not offer today.
  • You are an agency that wants a manager and client approval flow on a higher plan.

Pick LazyRelay if

  • You want to post to Telegram, Discord, Tumblr or Mastodon, none of which are named on SocialPilot's pages.
  • You want a free tier with no trial clock, and no card required.
  • You want independent proof that a post actually went live.
  • You want API access on every plan, not only an Enterprise plan.

Frequently asked questions

Does SocialPilot have a free plan?

Its pricing page shows a 14 day free trial on every plan with no card required, and no permanent free plan. LazyRelay's free tier has no time limit.

Does SocialPilot have MCP access for AI agents?

Yes. SocialPilot has a hosted MCP server that connects Claude and ChatGPT, included on every plan. LazyRelay also includes MCP access on every plan, including Free.

Does SocialPilot confirm a post is really live after publishing?

Its help center documents a Delivered tab for published posts and an Error tab for rejections. We did not find an independent check that the post is visible afterwards. LazyRelay's Proof of Publish does that on every plan.
