LazyRelay vs Sprout Social

Sprout Social is a premium, enterprise-leaning platform built around deep analytics and customer care. Here's an honest look at where it and LazyRelay actually differ.

Where Sprout Social is genuinely ahead

Sprout Social's "Trellis" AI agent, sentiment analysis, message-spike alerts, and social listening are far more developed than anything LazyRelay currently offers — it's built for teams that need deep customer-care and brand-monitoring tooling, not just scheduling.

The gap that actually matters: "sent" isn't "published"

Sprout Social's own marketing focuses on customer care, listening, and analytics, not on independently confirming a post is actually visible after it publishes. LazyRelay separately re-checks the platform after every post to confirm it's genuinely live — that's Proof-of-Publish, included on every LazyRelay plan including Free. We didn't find an equivalent check anywhere in Sprout Social's own documentation.

The other real difference: the one-year commitment

Sprout Social's paid plans require a one-year minimum commitment — you can trial for 30 days free, but subscribing locks you in for a year regardless of tier. LazyRelay has no annual commitment: Growth and Pro are billed monthly, Pay-as-you-go is a one time purchase, and the Free plan has no contract at all.

Pick Sprout Social if

  • Deep social listening, sentiment analysis, and customer-care workflows matter more to you than scheduling itself.
  • You're a larger team that can commit to an annual contract for the tooling.

Pick LazyRelay if

  • You want to try a real product for free, indefinitely, before paying anything.
  • You don't want to sign a one-year commitment just to use a scheduler.
  • You post to Telegram, Discord, Mastodon, Bluesky, or Tumblr.
  • You want independent proof a post actually went live, not just "accepted."

Try Proof-of-Publish for free. LazyRelay's free tier includes 30 credits once at registration.

Frequently asked questions

Is LazyRelay cheaper than Sprout Social?

Yes, substantially. Sprout Social's entry plan is $79/seat/month billed annually (5 social profiles), with no free plan, only a 30-day trial. LazyRelay's entry paid option is Pay-as-you-go at $15 one time (plus tax) for 300 credits, Growth is $29 a month (plus tax), and its Free plan is $0 with 30 credits once, with no annual commitment required.

Does Sprout Social have a free plan?

No. Sprout Social offers a 30-day free trial with no credit card required, but every paid plan requires a one-year minimum commitment. LazyRelay has a $0 Free plan with 30 credits and no commitment at all.

Does Sprout Social verify that a post actually went live?

Not that we found documented on sproutsocial.com. LazyRelay's Proof-of-Publish independently re-checks each platform after publishing to confirm the post is genuinely live, on every plan including Free.
