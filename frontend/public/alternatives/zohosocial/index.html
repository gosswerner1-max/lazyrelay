LazyRelay vs Zoho Social

Zoho Social is a broad suite with a free plan, an MCP server and a public API. Here is an honest look at where it and LazyRelay actually differ.

Where Zoho Social is genuinely ahead

Zoho Social lists a wide set of channels, including Google Business Profile, Mastodon, Bluesky, Threads and, on its Premium and Agency Plus plans, WhatsApp Business and Telegram Business. It also has an official MCP server for AI agents and a public REST API that covers drafts, scheduling, publishing, metrics and approvals. It is a serious competitor on AI agent access. If you already live in the Zoho suite, it fits naturally.

Pick Zoho Social if

  • You already use Zoho products and want your social tool in the same suite.
  • You need Google Business Profile, Snapchat or WhatsApp Business and Telegram Business on a higher plan.
  • You want a free plan with 6 channels and a public API.

Pick LazyRelay if

  • You want independent proof that a post actually went live, not only checks before publishing.
  • You want a free tier with 3 accounts and no card required.
  • You post to Discord, Tumblr, Lemmy, Slack, DEV, Hashnode or WordPress, which are not named on Zoho Social's pricing page.

Frequently asked questions

Does Zoho Social have MCP access for AI agents?

Yes. Zoho Social has an official MCP server for drafting, scheduling, publishing and monitoring posts. LazyRelay includes 27 MCP tools on every plan, including Free.

Does Zoho Social have a free plan?

Yes, after a 15 day trial: 1 brand, 6 channels, 1 team member and 5 AI credits. LazyRelay's free tier has 3 accounts, 250MB of storage and Proof of Publish.

Does Zoho Social check that a post is really live after publishing?

We did not find that on the pages we read. LazyRelay's Proof of Publish checks the platform again after every post, on every plan including Free.
