What LazyRelay can do

One place to schedule, publish and check your posts, with access for AI agents built in. Every line below describes something LazyRelay does today.

  • Publish

    18 distribution platforms plus WhatsApp

    Connect your accounts once and post to all of them from one screen, or from an AI agent.

    • Facebook
    • Instagram
    • TikTok
    • Pinterest
    • YouTube
    • LinkedIn
    • Threads
    • Mastodon
    • Bluesky
    • Telegram
    • Discord
    • Tumblr
    • WordPress
    • DEV
    • Hashnode
    • Lemmy
    • Slack
    • X

    X is available on paid plans, using your own X developer keys. WhatsApp is used for client messaging.

  • Schedule

    Scheduling that runs by itself

    Queue a one time post for the exact moment you want, or set a recurring weekly schedule once on a paid plan. Plan by day on the content calendar. LazyRelay checks for due posts every 30 seconds and sends them out when they are due.

  • Verify

    Proof of Publish

    After posting, LazyRelay looks at the platform again to confirm the post is really live, not just accepted. It is included on every plan, including Free.

  • AI agents

    27 MCP tools for AI agents

    LazyRelay works with any AI agent that supports MCP, including Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Cursor. Agents can check platform rules, create drafts, schedule, approve and read analytics. Use the hosted endpoint with sign in, or the local package with an API key. Included on every plan, including Free. See the integration guides.

  • Content Sources

    Content Sources: draft posts from your own website

    Connect an RSS feed or a sitemap from a website you have proven you own, and LazyRelay turns new pages into draft posts for you to review. Nothing is posted until you approve it.

  • Drafts

    Drafts for review

    See everything a source produced in one table, then review and edit, approve and move to the calendar, or dismiss each draft before anything is scheduled.

  • Privacy

    Page text is not kept

    When Content Sources builds a draft it keeps only the title and the link. The text of the page is used to write a short excerpt and is not stored by the service, and the database clears any stored page body once a draft has been handed to LazyRelay.

  • Security

    Your data stays yours

    Each customer can only reach their own sources and drafts, and the database enforces it with row level security. Your LazyRelay key is stored encrypted and is never shown again, only its last four characters. In our own testing with thousands of simulated cross customer attempts, none got through.

  • WhatsApp

    WhatsApp broadcasts

    Send a message to a list of contacts on WhatsApp. When a contact opts out, LazyRelay honours it and records it.

  • Review

    Approvals and client review links

    Mark a post as needing sign off before it goes out. On paid plans, send clients a review link so they can approve or ask for changes.

  • Teams

    Brands and team seats

    Keep several brands or clients in one account, with the number of brands and team seats depending on your plan.

  • Writing help

    AI captions, hashtags and ideas

    Generate wording or tags from your draft in one click and get fresh content ideas on demand. On paid plans, LazyRelay can also draft a suggested reply for Mastodon and Bluesky comments, and nothing posts until you approve it.

  • Bulk

    Bulk CSV import

    Schedule up to 200 posts at once, with a preview of every row before anything is saved.

  • Developers

    REST API and signed webhooks

    Create, schedule and manage posts through a REST API described by an OpenAPI document. Webhooks are signed with HMAC SHA256 and retried. Read the docs.

  • Insight

    Analytics dashboard

    See total posts, success rates for each platform and a daily volume trend.

  • Security

    Two factor authentication

    Turn on optional two factor sign in with an authenticator app, with one time recovery codes for safety.

Frequently asked questions

Which platforms can LazyRelay publish to?

LazyRelay publishes to 18 distribution platforms plus WhatsApp: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube, LinkedIn, Threads, Mastodon, Bluesky, Telegram, Discord, Tumblr, WordPress, DEV, Hashnode, Lemmy, Slack and X.

Does LazyRelay work with my AI agent?

LazyRelay works with any AI agent that supports MCP, including Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Cursor. It offers 27 MCP tools, included on every plan, through a hosted endpoint with sign in or a local package with an API key.

What is Proof of Publish?

Proof of Publish is a second check. After LazyRelay posts, it looks at the platform again to confirm the post is really live, instead of trusting that the platform accepted the request. It is included on every plan, including Free.

What does Content Sources do?

Content Sources reads an RSS feed or a sitemap from a website you have proven you own and turns new pages into draft posts for you to review. Nothing is posted until you approve it.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The free tier has 3 accounts and 250MB of storage, includes Proof of Publish and MCP access, and needs no card.
