Changelog

What's actually shipped, in plain language, most recent first.

August 10, 2026
  • Added a "first comment" option to the compose form for Facebook and Instagram, so a follow-up comment (handy for hashtags) posts automatically right after your post goes live.
  • Added a Guides section covering scheduler comparisons, platform coverage, and how several AI features actually work.
August 8, 2026
  • Shareable Proof-of-Publish links: turn any verified post into a public link you can send a client, franchise contact, or boss as proof it actually went live.
  • Optional email alerts the moment a post genuinely fails to publish, instead of only finding out by checking the dashboard.
  • AI-powered "needs attention" filter for Mentions and DMs, so you can see which comments and DMs are actually worth opening without reading the full list.
  • A webhook that fires the moment Proof-of-Publish confirms a post is live, for anyone wiring LazyRelay into their own tools.
  • "Not sure what to post? Get ideas" button in the compose form, generating post ideas grounded in your business and recent posts.
  • Brand labels for connected accounts, with filtering across every dashboard view, for anyone running more than one business through LazyRelay.
  • An on-demand AI insight on the Analytics tab comparing your best- and worst-performing posts.
August 7, 2026
  • Real engagement analytics (likes, comments, shares, views), not just post counts.
  • Comments inbox extended to Facebook and Instagram, with reply capability added across Facebook, Instagram, Mastodon, and Bluesky.
  • DM inbox for Facebook and Instagram.
  • DM automation: automatically send a DM to anyone who comments a specific keyword.
  • A public API and MCP server, plus a documentation page, for anyone who wants to schedule and manage posts programmatically or through an AI agent.
August 6, 2026
  • Threads posting confirmed live end to end.
  • Tumblr connected as a supported platform.
  • Added a "Disconnect" button for connected accounts.
  • Fixed a bug where a post's status sometimes didn't refresh automatically on the Overview tab.
  • Redesigned the Overview page with real charts instead of a plain list.
July 31, 2026
  • Analytics dashboard, Phase 1: total posts, per-platform success rates, and a daily volume trend.
  • Visual content calendar (month view).
  • Bulk CSV import: schedule up to 200 posts at once, with a per-row preview before importing.
  • AI caption generator.
  • Best-time-to-post suggestions, honestly labeled as general guidance, not personalized.
  • Approval workflow: mark a post as needing sign-off before it goes out.
  • AI hashtag suggestions.
  • Link-in-bio page.
  • Read-only comment tracking for YouTube, Mastodon, and Bluesky.
  • Browser extension for scheduling a page, link, or image directly from wherever you find it.
