Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated 20 October 2026

This page explains how to remove your data from LazyRelay, either a single connected platform, or your entire account. See our Privacy Policy for what we store and why.

Disconnect a single platform (self-serve)

To remove just one connected account (Meta, TikTok, Pinterest, or any other platform you've connected): go to your Dashboard, find the account under your connected platforms, and click Disconnect. This immediately revokes LazyRelay's access token for that platform and stops any future posts from being scheduled to it. Posts already published to that platform are not affected, since we only control what happens going forward.

Cancel your subscription (self-serve)

Go to the Billing tab of your Dashboard (under More in the top nav) and click Cancel. This stops future billing; your plan stays active until the end of the period you've already paid for. Your data is not deleted at that point, but 30 days after your subscription ends or is cancelled, your posts and uploaded media are permanently deleted. You'll confirm this when you cancel, and we'll email you a reminder about a week before it happens. Download anything you want to keep before then, or resubscribe before the 30 days are up to cancel the deletion.

When your data is deleted automatically

Subscription plans (Growth and Pro): data is deleted 30 days after the subscription ends or is cancelled, as described above.

Non-subscription plans (Free and Pay-as-you-go): Data is maintained as long as the account remains active, and is subject to deletion after 6 consecutive months of total inactivity.

Delete your entire account and data

LazyRelay doesn't yet have a self-serve "delete my account" button in the Dashboard. To request full deletion, email hello@lazyrelay.com from the email address on your account with the subject line "Delete my account." Once we confirm the request is really from you, we will, within a reasonable time:

  • Revoke every connected platform's access token, disconnecting all of your connected accounts.
  • Delete your account record, subscription and billing history, uploaded media, and scheduled/post history.
  • Cancel any active subscription, effective immediately rather than waiting out the billing period, since the account itself is being removed.

Records we're legally required to keep for tax/accounting purposes (e.g. sale and refund records for SARS bookkeeping) are retained as required by law even after account deletion, but are no longer linked to your connected-platform tokens or usable to access anything on your behalf.

How long it takes

Platform disconnects and subscription cancellations you trigger yourself in the Dashboard take effect immediately. Full account-deletion requests sent by email are processed within a reasonable time after we've verified the request. If it's been more than a few business days without a reply, follow up on the same email thread.

Contact us

Questions about deleting your data, or a request in progress? Email hello@lazyrelay.com.
