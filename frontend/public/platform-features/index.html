This table covers the 12 social platforms LazyRelay posts to. Separately, every paid plan also includes a Zapier integration and API access for connecting LazyRelay to non-social apps — Google Sheets, Slack, Shopify, WordPress, and 9,000+ others.

Why the gaps exist

Facebook and Instagram share the same underlying Meta API, which is the most complete of any platform for comments, DMs, and first-comment posting, so those two get the fullest feature set. Mastodon and Bluesky expose enough of their comment and metrics APIs without needing extra permissions, so they get analytics and comment replies but not DM features, which those platforms don't expose the same way. YouTube exposes comments and metrics but not comment replies, since that needs a scope this app hasn't requested yet. The remaining platforms (TikTok, Pinterest, LinkedIn, Threads, Telegram, Discord, Tumblr) currently only expose posting through their public APIs, not comment or DM management, which is a limit of what those platforms allow, not something left unbuilt.
