Frequently asked questions

Is LazyRelay actually free?

Yes. The Free plan is $0, no credit card required, and includes 3 connected accounts, 1 brand, 250MB of storage, and Proof-of-Publish verification on every post. It's limited to one-time scheduled posts and 10 posts per account (refillable), rather than unlimited or recurring scheduling.

What does LazyRelay's pricing actually cost per platform?

Nothing extra. LazyRelay charges one flat price per tier, not a fee per connected platform or account. Starter is $29.99/month, Pro is $59.99/month, Business is $99.99/month, and Agency plans run $149.99 and $199.99/month, each with a higher connected-account limit, not a per-platform surcharge.

Is AI-agent and MCP access included, or is it a paid add-on?

It's included on every paid plan (Starter through Agency Plus) at no extra cost. It is not available on the Free plan.

What if I need more storage, brands, or team seats than my plan includes?

Paid plans support storage, brand, and team-seat add-ons, so you can extend a plan you're otherwise happy with instead of being forced to jump to the next full tier.
