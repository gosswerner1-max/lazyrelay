What a credit pays for

  • Publishing: 1 credit per channel destination. Posting to 5 channels: 5 channels × 1 credit = 5 credits.
  • Competitor page teardown: 10 credits. Charged only when the blueprint is delivered. A failed teardown costs nothing.
  • A channel that fails is not charged. A credit is taken only after the platform confirms the post is live (Proof-of-Publish).
  • Credits that expire first are spent first, then credits that never expire.
  • When your credits run out, due posts wait. They are not deleted, and they publish once you add credits.

Frequently asked questions

Is LazyRelay actually free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and gives you 30 credits once, when you register. There is no monthly reset. Free allows 3 connected accounts, 1 brand and 10 queued posts. Every published post carries Proof-of-Publish verification.

How much does LazyRelay cost?

Pay-as-you-go is $15 one time for 300 credits that stay in your wallet until used. Balance can be topped up at any time. Growth is $29 a month with 1,000 credits each billing cycle. Pro is $99 a month with 6,000 credits each billing cycle. Free costs $0 and includes 30 credits once.

What does a credit pay for?

One credit pays for one channel destination. A post sent to 5 channels costs 5 credits. A competitor page teardown costs 10 credits. A credit is taken for a post only after the platform confirms it is live, so a channel that fails is not charged. A failed teardown costs nothing.

Do credits expire?

Free credits, Pay-as-you-go credits, Starter pack credits and Growth pack credits never expire. Growth and Pro plan credits expire at the end of each billing cycle, with no rollover. Pro top-up credits are bound to the Pro subscription and expire if it is canceled. A cancel you choose yourself keeps the plan until the end of the paid period. Credits that expire first are spent first, then credits that never expire.

What credit packs can I buy?

Three packs are available. The Starter pack is $15 for 300 credits and the Growth pack is $50 for 1,500 credits, and their credits never expire. The Pro top-up is $30 for 1,500 credits (bound to active subscription status, expires if canceled). It is available only to active Pro subscribers.

What happens when my credits run out?

Posts that are due wait. They are not deleted. They publish once you add credits.

Is AI-agent and MCP access included, or is it a paid add-on?

AI-agent and MCP access is available on every plan, including Free, at no extra cost.

Does LazyRelay integrate with non-social apps, not just social media platforms?

Yes. LazyRelay has a Zapier integration that connects it to 9,000+ other apps (Google Sheets, Slack, Shopify, WordPress, and more), plus direct API access for custom integrations. This is separate from the 18 distribution platforms plus WhatsApp that LazyRelay connects to.

How does LazyRelay's Proof-of-Publish verification differentiate it from typical social media schedulers like Buffer or Hootsuite?

A scheduler that only records the platform's initial "accepted" response can tell you a post was sent, but not that it actually went live. LazyRelay doesn't stop at that response: right after publishing, it independently checks with the platform that the post is live, and shows you the result per post. Proof-of-Publish is included on every plan, including Free. For how specific tools compare, see the comparison pages at lazyrelay.com/alternatives/.

What security infrastructure does LazyRelay use to safely connect and manage multiple brand accounts simultaneously?

Sign-in and passwords are handled by Supabase Auth, so LazyRelay never stores your password, and optional two-factor authentication (TOTP) is available. The access tokens for your connected social accounts are encrypted in Supabase Vault. Every database table has row-level security so one customer's data is not reachable by another's, API routes are rate limited, and the site is served over HTTPS with HSTS.
