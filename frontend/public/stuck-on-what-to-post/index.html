Stuck on What to Post? Most Schedulers Won't Help With That.

Every social media scheduler assumes you already know what to post and just need help getting it out the door. Here's what happens when that's not actually the hard part, and what a scheduler that helps with the idea itself looks like.

The problem before the scheduling problem

Nearly every social media tool solves the same problem: you have a post ready, and you need it to go out at the right time, to the right accounts, reliably. That's a real problem, and scheduling tools are genuinely good at it.

It just isn't the problem a lot of people actually get stuck on. For a solo business owner or a small team, the harder moment usually comes earlier: staring at a blank compose box with no idea what to write about today. Scheduling doesn't help with that. Neither does a content calendar, or an analytics dashboard, or an AI caption generator, since all of those assume you already know your topic and just need help with what comes after.

Why most tools skip straight past this

It's a reasonable place for a roadmap to stop. A scheduler's core job is getting the post out reliably, and that's already a real, well-defined problem to solve. "Help me figure out what to say in the first place" is a fuzzier, more subjective ask, and it's easy to assume the customer has already solved it before they ever open the app.

For a lot of small business owners posting for themselves, that assumption doesn't hold. The blank box is the actual barrier, more often than the scheduling logistics around it.

What a starting point actually looks like

LazyRelay's compose form has a "Not sure what to post? Get ideas" button sitting right above the topic field, not a separate tool you have to go find. It generates five varied post ideas grounded in your business and your own recent posting history, so the suggestions stay relevant and don't repeat what you already covered last week. Click one, and it drops straight into the topic field, ready to turn into a real post.

It's not trying to write the post for you end to end. It's aimed at the specific moment where most people actually get stuck: not knowing where to start.
