How to Use This Planner

  3. Log every seller, landlord and buyer conversation straight away.
  4. Follow up on the date you wrote down — the follow-up is where listings are won.
  5. Review weekly and monthly: keep what produces results, drop what does not.

Daily Working Routine

Daily Working Routine

A rhythm that puts listing activity first

08:00

Plan and prepare

Review the daily schedule, outstanding tasks and address route.

09:00

Find potential sellers

Make calls, respond to enquiries and identify potential listing opportunities.

11:00

Work the area

Visit planned addresses and attend appointments.

13:00

Buyers and properties

Manage property information, buyer requirements and viewings.

15:00

Follow-ups and review

Update records, contact clients, confirm tomorrow's appointments and review the day's results.

These times are examples and can be adjusted to suit your agency and appointments.

My own routine (adjust to suit)

TimeFocusWhy it matters

Final purpose

This planner is designed to become your everyday real estate business management tool. It connects your address visits, seller leads, buyer requirements, property viewings and follow-ups in one organised system.

The main objective is to help you stay consistent, build relationships, find more potential listings, improve your client service and grow your real estate business.

South African Compliance Checklist

Keep your records and conduct in order

Compliance reminder checklist

  • Confirm authority to market the property
  • Verify owner or authorised representative details
  • Confirm listing status and approved asking price
  • Check required disclosures and records
  • Ensure advertising is accurate and not misleading
  • Obtain necessary permissions for access and photographs
  • Follow applicable FICA and verification procedures
  • Protect personal and client information
  • Record instructions and important communications
  • Refer compliance questions to the principal or compliance officer
