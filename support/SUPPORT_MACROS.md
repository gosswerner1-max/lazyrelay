# LAZYRELAY SUPPORT MACROS

## MACRO 1 GENERAL REFUND DENIAL
Subject: Update regarding your LazyRelay billing inquiry
Wording: Thank you for reaching out to LazyRelay support. We have reviewed your account configuration and transaction history. Per our locked platform terms and billing documentation, all credit package purchases, top-ups, and active subscription cycles are strictly non-refundable. Except as required under local consumer protection laws or our payment processor's mandatory terms. Because our credits do not have an expiration date for Pay-As-You-Go accounts, your balance remains entirely safe while your account stays active and usable in your wallet whenever you choose to resume regular posting activities. We appreciate your understanding. Let us know if you have any questions regarding your active credit limits or brand settings.

## MACRO 2 STATUTORY ESCALATION
Subject: Review of your regional billing inquiry
Wording: Thank you for your follow-up. As stated in our platform policies, our core billing structure operates under a strictly non-refundable model. Except as required under local consumer protection laws or our payment processor's mandatory terms. If your transaction falls within the legally mandated consumer protection window for your region, the process will follow the mandatory guidelines via the payment network to your original payment method. You will receive an updated transaction confirmation once the review is completed.

## MACRO 3 DELETION WARNING
Subject: Important information regarding your LazyRelay workspace data
Wording: To ensure maximum performance and security across the LazyRelay network, our data retention policies are structured based on account type. For Subscription Plans like Growth and Pro, data is slated for deletion 30 days after a subscription ends or cancels. For Non-Subscription Plans like Free and Pay-As-You-Go, your credit balances never expire. However, workspace data is maintained only as long as the account remains active, and is subject to deletion only after 6 consecutive months of total inactivity. To keep your workspace data completely secure and active, simply log into your dashboard at your convenience. No credit deductions or active posts are required to maintain your active status.
